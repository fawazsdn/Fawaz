Account Deletion

Last updated: 2026-10-14

You can delete your Haratna account at any time from inside the app.

How

  • Open Haratna and go to Profile → Settings.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.
  • Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photo, interests, privacy settings and date-of-birth declaration.
  • Posts, comments, polls, activities you host, listings, help requests, lost & found posts and issue reports you created.
  • Messages you sent, reports you filed, neighbor connections and requests, invitations and uploaded photos.
  • Clubs you organize pass to an adult member or are archived.

If you can't open the app

Contact the in-app Contact support form from the email address of your account and we will delete it for you.
