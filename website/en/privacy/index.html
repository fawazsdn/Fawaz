Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

This policy explains what Haratna ("we") collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and how you can control it. Haratna is a neighborhood app for residents in Saudi Arabia. It is offered by the Haratna team. This policy is prepared with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) in mind and is subject to legal review.

1. Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password (the password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider). If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the identifier and the name/email that provider shares with us; with Apple you can hide your email.
  • Profile: first name, last name, how your name is shown (full, first name + initial, or first name only), an optional bio and an optional profile photo.
  • Neighborhood: the region, city and neighborhood you choose from the official Saudi address dataset.
  • Date of birth (only if you choose to create a club): it is self-declared, stored privately, never shown to other residents and only used to check that club organizers are 18 or older. It is not identity verification.
  • Interests you select (for example padel or reading) and your Neighbors privacy settings.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, polls and votes, activities and RSVPs, club applications, announcements and chat messages, marketplace and borrow listings, help requests, lost & found posts (including any contact method you choose to add), issue reports, recommendations, photos, reports you file and support requests.

2. Location

  • To verify that you live in a neighborhood, the app asks for your location once, only when you tap Verify ("while using the app" permission). Your coordinates are sent to our server only to check them against the official neighborhood boundary; we store the result (verified or not), not the coordinates.
  • When you choose to pin an activity, post or listing on the map, we store an approximate point rounded to about 100 meters, never your exact position.
  • We do not track your location in the background.

3. Information created when you use Haratna

  • Neighbor connections and requests, club memberships and roles, activity invitations, blocks and mutes, notifications, reputation points and referral codes.
  • Technical data needed to run the service, such as authentication sessions and server logs kept by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.
  • We do not use advertising, we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the service: show your neighborhood's content, connect neighbors, run clubs and activities, deliver messages and notifications.
  • To keep the community safe: neighborhood verification, the 18+ rule for club organizers, content filtering, reports, blocking, moderation and preventing abuse.
  • To answer support requests and to meet legal obligations.

5. Who can see what

  • Neighborhood content (posts, activities, listings, approved clubs) is visible only to verified residents of the same neighborhood.
  • You do not appear in Discover Neighbors unless you turn on "Allow neighbors to find me" (off by default). Your neighbor list is private to you.
  • Private messages are visible only to the people in the conversation. Club chats are visible only to active members.
  • We never show your address, exact location, phone number, email address or date of birth to other residents.
  • Haratna staff (moderators and administrators) can access content when needed to review reports, review club applications, answer support requests or enforce our guidelines.

6. Service providers

We share data only with providers that run the service for us, under agreements that require them to protect it at least as strongly as this policy:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage and server functions. Data location: available on request through support.
  • Apple and Google — only if you choose their sign-in.
  • Apple Maps (on iPhone) — to display maps; map requests do not include your account information.
  • Email delivery provider — to send verification and password-reset emails: available on request through support.

7. How long we keep data

  • Your account and content are kept while your account is active.
  • When you delete content, it is removed from the app. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, your posts, comments, messages you sent, activities you host, listings, requests, reports you filed, uploaded photos, connections, interests, settings and your date-of-birth declaration. Clubs you organize pass to an adult member or are archived.
  • Copies in encrypted backups kept by our hosting provider are deleted on its backup schedule: available on request through support.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correct your profile at any time in Settings → Edit profile.
  • Control discovery, neighbor requests, messages, interests and what others can see in Settings → Neighbors & privacy.
  • Withdraw location permission at any time in iPhone Settings; already-verified membership is not affected.
  • Delete your account inside the app: Settings → Delete account. This cannot be undone.
  • Ask us a question or request a copy of your data through Settings → Contact support or the in-app Contact support form. You may also contact the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA) regarding personal data protection.

9. Children

Haratna is a community app for residents and is not designed for young children. Residents under 18 cannot create, own or run clubs. Parents and guardians who believe a child has provided information can contact us to have it removed.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by access rules enforced on our servers (for example, residents can only read their own neighborhood's content). No system is perfectly secure; please report any concern through support.

11. Changes

We will update this page and the date below when the policy changes, and tell you in the app about significant changes.

12. Contact

the Haratna team · the in-app Contact support form · In the app: Settings → Contact support.
