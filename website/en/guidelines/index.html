Community Guidelines

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Haratna works when neighbors treat each other the way they would face to face. These guidelines apply to everything in the app: posts, comments, messages, clubs, activities, listings and profiles.

Be a good neighbor

  • Be respectful, even when you disagree.
  • Post things that are useful or relevant to your neighborhood.
  • Use your real name and a genuine photo, or none.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech or insults about religion, origin, gender, disability or any group.
  • Sexual content, nudity, violence or graphic material.
  • Sharing anyone's private information: home address, exact location, phone number, documents or photos without consent.
  • Scams, spam, misleading offers or unauthorized commercial promotion.
  • Illegal goods or activities, or content that breaks Saudi law.
  • Impersonating someone or misrepresenting where you live.

Safety when meeting

  • Meet in public places such as parks, mosques' public areas or community centers.
  • Children's activities must be organized by adults and attended with guardians.
  • Never send money in advance to someone you have not met.

Report, block, move on

Use Report on any post, comment, message, profile, activity or club. Use Block on a profile to stop someone contacting you; blocking also removes them as a neighbor. Our team reviews reports and aims to act within 24 hours. Repeated or serious violations lead to removal from Haratna.
