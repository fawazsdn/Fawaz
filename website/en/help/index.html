Help Center

Why do I need to verify my neighborhood?

Haratna shows each neighborhood's content only to people who live there. Verification checks your location once against the official neighborhood boundary. Your coordinates are not stored.

How do Neighbors work?

Open a resident's profile and tap Add Neighbor. You become neighbors only when they accept. You can message connected neighbors. You appear in Discover Neighbors only if you turn on "Allow neighbors to find me" in Settings → Neighbors & privacy.

How do I create a club?

Go to Create → Create Club. Club organizers must be 18 or older. Every club is reviewed by the Haratna team before neighbors can see it.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap ⋯ on a profile, post, comment, message or club and choose Report. On a profile choose Block to stop all contact.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. Your profile, content, messages you sent and photos are deleted. This cannot be undone.

Contact support

In the app: Settings → Contact support. Email: the in-app Contact support form.
