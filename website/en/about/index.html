About Haratna

Haratna (حارتنا, "our neighborhood") connects verified residents of the same Saudi neighborhood: local news and questions, activities and events, clubs, a neighborhood marketplace, help between neighbors, lost & found and issue reports — in Arabic and English.

Each neighborhood is private to its verified residents. Discovery of neighbors is opt-in, and your exact location is never shown.
